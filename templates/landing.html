Why RecoverAI

Everything you need to recover more,

ML Recovery Scoring

Gradient-boosted model on 20K labeled payment records predicts each failed transaction's recovery probability before any action is taken.

Policy-Enforced Orchestration

Recovery actions flow through a clinical decision engine — retry, payment link, reminder, escalate — gated by business rules and human approval.

Payment Gateway Connect

Real Razorpay integration for retries and payment links, with fully-labeled simulation mode so every action is transparently classified.

Hash-Chained Ledger

Every recovery event is appended to a cryptographically hash-chained ledger and independently verifiable, preventing silent tampering.

Role-Based Access Control

Merchant admins and reviewers get scoped permissions enforced at the API level — approvals, audit access, and destructive actions guarded.

Full Audit Trail

Immutable audit logs capture every prediction, approval, recovery, and system event with actor attribution and timestamps.
